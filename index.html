How we work

No flour to sell, no health claims

Olivaraa sells nothing: no starters, no bannetons, no flour, no affiliate links. What you own is almost certainly enough, and a Dutch oven is the only purchase we ever push.

We also make no health claims. Sourdough is bread, not medicine, and it is not safe for anyone with celiac disease — long fermentation does not remove gluten. If you want to sell what you bake, cottage food rules vary by state and that is a conversation for your local health department.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

My starter is dead.

Almost certainly not. Cold kitchens and irregular feeding explain nearly every 'dead' starter, and two days of consistent feeding usually settles it.

Do I need a stand mixer?

No. Hand mixing is fine and arguably better for learning what the dough should feel like. We sell nothing and recommend very little.

Is sourdough gluten-free or healthier?

No, and we don't make health claims. It is not safe for celiac disease. Enjoy it as bread.

Why is my loaf gummy inside?

Usually underfermented, sometimes cut too warm. A photo of the crumb will tell us which within a day.

Can I sell my bread?

Possibly, under your state's cottage food laws — but that's a question for your local health department, not for us. We coach baking, not food business compliance.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.
